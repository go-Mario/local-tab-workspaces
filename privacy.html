Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 21, 2026 · Product: Local Tab Workspaces

This Privacy Policy explains how Local Tab Workspaces (“the Product”, “we”, “I”) handles information. The Product is a local-first Chrome extension. The website and Creem store / Early Access offer are operated by LIAO XIANG, an individual indie developer based in China (legal name matching merchant onboarding).

1. What the Product stores locally

Core tab workspace data (saved tab groups, restore settings, and related local preferences) is stored on your device using browser storage. We do not sell this data. Local use does not require an account.

2. Waitlist email

If you enter an email on the Early Access waitlist form on this website, that address is currently kept only in your browser’s local storage for the demo form (it is not uploaded to our servers yet). If a server-backed waitlist is added later, we will use your email only to notify you about Early Access and product updates, and will not sell it.

3. Payments (Creem)

If you purchase the $1 Early Access seat hold, payment is processed by Creem (Merchant of Record). We do not receive or store your full card details. Creem’s privacy practices apply to checkout data. Checkout: https://www.creem.io/payment/prod_1sxcev5EyzFKkDzSRj3ZSs.

4. Optional sync (future)

Cross-device sync is an optional paid feature planned for later. If you enable sync, workspace data needed for that feature may be transmitted to our sync service under your account. Local save/restore remains available without sync.

5. No sale of personal data

We do not sell personal data. We do not use waitlist or support emails for advertising lists.
